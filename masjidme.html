MasjidMe Features

Everything around your masjid life.

A complete feature direction for mosque discovery, Islamic content, community alerts, and admin-controlled mosque updates.

01

Nearby Masjid Map

Users can discover nearby mosques, view mosque profiles, opening information, contact details, distance, directions, and community information through a map-focused experience.

02

Prayer Times & Azan

Prayer time display with notification planning for Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha. Designed to support local reminders and mosque-centered prayer information.

03

Masjid Announcements

Mosques can publish short updates such as program notices, Friday announcements, community reminders, class cancellations, donation alerts, and emergency notices.

04

Events & Classes

Community members can find kuliah, classes, lectures, youth programs, family events, Ramadan programs, volunteering activities, and registration information.

05

Sermons & Khutbah

Masjid admins can share khutbah, sermons, audio recordings, YouTube live links, and useful Islamic reminders so users can revisit content after the program.

06

Quran & Doa

The Islamic library direction includes Quran reading, translation, recitation support, selected doa, Malay/Rumi support, and useful references for daily Muslim life.

07

Halal Places

Users can discover halal restaurants and places, with future support for verified listings, community reporting, and trusted information connected to local needs.

08

Certificates

The Sijil Saya feature can generate participation certificates for mosque classes or events, with downloadable/shareable certificate designs and MasjidMe branding.

09

Admin & Superadmin

Masjid admins manage mosque content, while superadmin approval helps keep mosque profiles trusted, organized, and suitable for a larger mosque network.

MasjidMe Privacy Policy

Privacy and data handling for MasjidMe

MasjidMe is designed as a community and mosque information platform. We aim to collect only the information needed to provide features such as nearby masjid discovery, prayer reminders, announcements, mosque profiles, events, classes, sermons, feedback, and admin tools.

Information MasjidMe may use

  • Location permission: Used only when you choose features such as nearby masjid discovery, map view, distance calculation, or directions.
  • Notification permission: Used for optional prayer reminders, mosque announcements, event reminders, class updates, or community alerts.
  • Account/admin information: Mosque admins or superadmins may provide names, emails, mosque role information, and mosque profile data for approval and management.
  • Mosque and community content: Announcements, events, classes, sermons, certificate records, volunteer posts, halal listings, and feedback may be stored to provide the app experience.
  • App diagnostics: Crash logs and performance data may be used to improve stability and fix bugs.

How MasjidMe uses information

  • To show nearby mosques and relevant local information.
  • To send optional reminders and notifications chosen by the user.
  • To allow mosque admins to publish trusted community content.
  • To improve app safety, reliability, moderation, and user experience.
  • To respond to support, privacy, or account-related requests.

What MasjidMe does not do

  • MasjidMe does not sell personal data.
  • MasjidMe does not collect payment card details directly; payments, if added, are handled by Apple, Google, or approved payment providers.
  • MasjidMe does not use precise location in the background unless a clear future feature requires it and the user gives permission.
  • MasjidMe does not publish private user information as public mosque content without a user/admin action.

Third-party services

MasjidMe may use services such as Firebase, Google Maps, Google Places, push notification services, app store billing systems, and Islamic content APIs. These services may process limited technical data according to their own privacy policies.

Children and families

MasjidMe is intended as a family-friendly Islamic community app. If children use the app, parents or guardians should supervise usage, especially where community content, location, or online features are involved.

Contact

For privacy questions, mosque profile concerns, admin access issues, or deletion requests, contact Greeny App Studio at support@greenystudio.site.
